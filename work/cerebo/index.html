The day’s operations, on one screen.

Cerebo is a web dashboard for managing supply operations on the Motion Track platform: real-time fleet tracking, workforce performance, re-routing and delivery-route optimisation, with estimated times of arrival recalculated from conditions actually on the ground.

The team is eighty-plus, so most of the work is making one screen fast enough that planners stop keeping a second list beside it.

Role
Frontend Engineer
Dates
Sep 2018 – Jul 2019
Surface
Live fleet tracking, orders timeline, API monitoring
Stack
React, Python, AWS, Nginx

The problem

A planner’s day has three clocks: what was planned, what is happening now, and what the customer was promised. When those live in three views, reconciliation happens by memory.

The dashboard had to hold all three without making the planner zoom.

Three views, one day.

Approach
I

Live fleet tracking

Positions update as they arrive, with the day’s operations next to them, so “where is it” and “what next” answer together.

II

Gantt of orders

A timeline of planned tours against real-time execution. Late starts and slipped tours are visible as shape, not as a column of timestamps.

III

ELTCA, the API watch

A monitoring app that reports API outages to development and customer support in one place, so the pre-check on a failure happens before the customer has to describe it.

Outcome

One screenplan and execution
Real timefleet positions
ETAsfrom on-ground conditions
Two teamsdev and support, one alert
