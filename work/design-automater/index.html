A day of listing work in about an hour.

Designers were spending their afternoons re-typing the same product data into marketplace forms. Design Automater gives them one small interface for preparing a listing, and pushes the result to Amazon and Flipkart automatically, with inventory kept in step.

Role
Technical Lead
Dates
Jun – Dec 2016
Surface
Listing editor, inventory sync
Result
About an hour instead of a day

The problem

Marketplace forms are repetitive by nature: the same attributes, retyped per channel, each with its own field limits. Every extra field the designer must learn is a reason to go back to a spreadsheet.

The tool had to be smaller than the form it replaced.

Small editor, loud defaults.

Approach
I

One entry per attribute

Type it once; each channel trims and maps it on the way out. Limits are shown as hints, not as blocked fields.

II

Designers first

The editing screen leads with the image and the copy, since that is what a designer actually changes. Identifiers sit in a compact strip below.

III

Sync with receipts

Every push to a marketplace logs what was sent and what came back, so a silent failure becomes a row someone can read.

Outcome

About 1 hourfor a full day of listings
Two channelsAmazon and Flipkart
Logged pusheswith responses
Single entryper attribute

“Our designers stopped treating listing updates as a day of admin.”
